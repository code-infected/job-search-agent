JobsSetup

Set up your job search

Everything stays on this computer, except what is sent to your model provider to score postings and write drafts.

    1. Model

    The AI model that scores postings and writes drafts. You pay your provider for its use.

    2. About you

    The agent only ever says what is written here. Upload your resume to get suggestions, then check every one: fix anything wrong, and delete anything you would not want quoted.

    3. What you are looking for

    In plain words: role types, your experience level, where you can work, and anything that rules a job out. The model scores every posting against this.

    Comma separated. Leave empty to consider every title.

    Comma separated. A posting open worldwide always passes. Leave empty for anywhere.

    4. Where to look

    Use any mix of the four below. Every run checks all of them, and filters every title by step 3 before any model call, so a wide search costs little.

    Job sites

    Sites listing jobs from many employers. Ticked sites are searched with these terms on every run. Each is credited and linked on the Jobs page, as its terms ask.

    Comma separated, up to ten. Leave empty to use the title words from step 3.

    Used by the sites that search one country or city. Others ignore it.

    Every company on a job board system

    Most companies post jobs on one of these systems. The agent can check every company it knows on them, a slice per run, oldest first. The company list comes from Common Crawl, a public archive of the web, and is refreshed monthly. Needs title words in step 3.

    A specific company

    Type a company name, or paste its careers page link. The agent finds which job board it uses, including Workday careers sites.

    Companies and postings added

    Keys for more job sites

    Optional. These sites need a free key from their own website. Keys are kept in .env on this computer and never shown again.

    5. Preferences

    From 0 to 1. Lower it to see more borderline matches.

    While this app is open. New jobs appear on the Jobs page.

    From your provider's pricing page. Without prices, only the token limit applies and cost is not shown.

    Score old jobs again

    After you change your facts or what you are looking for, the agent can look again at jobs it ruled out. Each one costs a model call on the next run. Jobs you approved or rejected are kept.
