JobsSetup

Set up your job search

Everything stays on this computer, except what is sent to your model provider to score postings and write drafts.

    1. Model

    The AI model that scores postings and writes drafts. You pay your provider for its use.

    2. About you

    The agent only ever says what is written here. Upload your resume to get suggestions, then check every one: fix anything wrong, and delete anything you would not want quoted.

    3. What you are looking for

    In plain words: role types, your experience level, where you can work, and anything that rules a job out. The model scores every posting against this.

    Comma separated. Leave empty to consider every title.

    Comma separated. A posting open worldwide always passes. Leave empty for anywhere.

    4. Where to look

    Add companies you would like to work for, and searches across many employers.

    Type a company name, or paste its careers page link. The agent finds which job board it uses.

    Searches Remotive. It updates a few times a day, so the agent checks it at most every six hours.

    Searches Adzuna, which lists jobs from many sites. It needs a free key from developer.adzuna.com, entered under Preferences. Adzuna shares only a short summary of each job, so scores are rougher.

    5. Preferences

    From 0 to 1. Lower it to see more borderline matches.

    While this app is open. New jobs appear on the Jobs page.

    From your provider's pricing page. Without prices, only the token limit applies and cost is not shown.

    Score old jobs again

    After you change your facts or what you are looking for, the agent can look again at jobs it ruled out. Each one costs a model call on the next run. Jobs you approved or rejected are kept.
